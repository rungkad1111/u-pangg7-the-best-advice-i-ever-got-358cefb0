## Entry

> My grandfather told me never to lend money I couldn't afford to lose, and I have never lost a friend over a loan since.

Length: 119 characters (gate: 1–200 characters). One sentence.

## Why this sentence

A named giver, a real moment, advice worth carrying: a rule about money that is really a rule about friendship. The payoff — never having lost a friend over a loan since — is the proof the advice was lived, not just heard. It is tight, reads in one breath, and leaves the reader with something to keep after the last word.
